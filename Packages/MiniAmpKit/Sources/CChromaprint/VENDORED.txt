Chromaprint 1.6.2, from its release tag:

  https://github.com/acoustid/chromaprint/archive/refs/tags/v1.6.2.tar.gz
    SHA-256 7f92ea1141429b43f695a1ba56d5058132f89fae5f137ce705afc57734f6b5fa

Copied unmodified from the tarball's src/: the sources its CMake builds on macOS with vDSP and
the internal resampler (audio_processor, chroma, chroma_resampler, chroma_filter, spectrum,
fft, fingerprinter, image_builder, simhash, silence_remover, fingerprint_calculator,
fingerprint_compressor, fingerprint_decompressor, fingerprinter_configuration,
fingerprint_matcher, chromaprint, fft_lib_vdsp, utils/base64, avresample/resample2.c), and the
headers they use. include/chromaprint.h is the tarball's src/include/chromaprint.h.
Left out: the FFmpeg, FFTW, and KissFFT back ends, tests, the command-line tool, and CMake files.

Written for this build in place of what CMake generates: src/config.h.
Licence: LICENSE.md. Chromaprint is MIT; the included FFmpeg resampler (src/avresample) is
LGPL 2.1, so the whole is LGPL 2.1, like TagLib.
